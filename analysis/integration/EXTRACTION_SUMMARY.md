## 🔀 Method Convergence Scoring

**Convergence = how many methods agree that a gene is post-transcriptionally regulated**

### Three methods:
1. **Kinetics:** Bayesian hierarchical kinetic model flags genes with amplitude divergence (|a| ≠ 1)
2. **Distance:** RNA-protein profile discordance (d_full > 75th percentile)
3. **ML:** Protein unpredictable from RNA alone (R² < 25th percentile)

### Convergence levels:
- **3 (All methods agree):** Highest-confidence PTM candidates (~2-3% of genes)
- **2 (Two methods):** Secondary candidates (~8%)
- **1 (One method):** Lower-confidence (~14%)
- **0 (No agreement):** No PTM signal (~76%)
